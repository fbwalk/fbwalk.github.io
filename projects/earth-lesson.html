Exploring the Omnivore's Dilemma

Students use Google Earth to explore the locations Michael Pollan visits in his book the Omnivore's Dilemma.

Open lesson in Google Earth

Opens in a new tab. Google Earth lessons can't be embedded directly in other web pages.

Overview

Designed for 8th Grade Humanities and Social Studies focused on learning about food and sustainabilty. Pairs with the young readers edition of the Omnivore's Dilemma.

What students do

Over one 60 minute class period, students explore the three main meals from the book. They look back in time to see landscape change in different American farms, explore street view imagry to view agricultural landscapes up close, and compare industrial food systems to local alternatives.

Materials

Use this worksheet and slide deck to walk students through the exercise. Students do not need to be familiar with the book to explore and learn more about our agricultural landscape.
