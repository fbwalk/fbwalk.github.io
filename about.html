About

I am an educator passionate about the relationships between people and place. Currently I am a Teacher in Training in the Sun Valley Community School's Teacher Training Academy, working with their 8th Grade Humanities class. In the summers I work in Outdoor Education with the Colorado Outward Bound School. I graduated from Middlebury College in February 2025 with a joint degree in Geography and Environmental Studies. In college and in my free-time I use GIS, data science, and cartography to tell complex stories about human-environment relationships.
